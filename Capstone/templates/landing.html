Children fall behind on their schedules — and no one knows until it's too late.

Across San Jacinto's {{ barangay_count }} barangays, health workers track hundreds of children using paper records. When a child misses a dose, the gap often goes undetected until the window for catch-up has already closed.

ImmunoVision was built to fix exactly that — checking each child's history against all {{ total_doses }} routine doses to flag risk before it becomes a missed vaccine.

How ImmunoVision works

From paper records to proactive follow-up, in four steps.

1

Import records

The RHU encodes or imports each child's immunization record into the system.

2

Score each child

ImmunoVision checks every child's vaccination history against the DOH EPI schedule.

3

Flag at-risk children

Children drifting off schedule surface on the dashboard before their catch-up window closes.

4

Health workers act

Barangay health workers follow up with flagged children and update the record.
