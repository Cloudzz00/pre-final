How Each Method Allocates
The system computes all three; the decision stays with the RHU
Priority first, then proportional

Requests marked Urgent are filled first, then High, then Normal. Within the same priority, the larger request is served first. Uses the urgency the requesting health worker set.

Proportional to request

Every barangay receives the same share of what it asked for. Fair between requesters, but takes no account of which barangay needs it most.

Lowest coverage first

Barangays furthest below the 95% target are filled first. Closes coverage gaps fastest, but a barangay that is performing well may receive nothing.

What the suggestion will not do

No barangay is allocated more than it requested. Whole vials only, and the totals always match the stock available: no vial is left unassigned or double-counted.
