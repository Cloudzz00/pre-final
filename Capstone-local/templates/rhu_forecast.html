Births Recorded Per Year
The basis for the cohort estimate
How This Is Calculated
Two components, only one of which is estimated
Already due

Doses that children currently in the registry will become due for in that year, calculated from each child's date of birth against the DOH EPI schedule. This is measured, not predicted.

Projected new

Doses for children not yet born or registered. Based on {{ cohort.low }} to {{ cohort.high }} births per year, the average of {{ cohort.years|join(', ') }} with a 15% upper margin.

Why an average and not a trend

Fitting a line to this registry produces an implausible rise, because the earliest years hold only a handful of records: a registry being set up, not a change in births. The average of recent complete years is the defensible estimate.

What this does not include

No wastage allowance, since that factor is set by the RHU's own practice and vial sizes. Add it to these figures when ordering.
