Built for the RHU's workflow

What the system does, matched to how immunization work already runs in San Jacinto.

{{ icon('map', 20) }}

Barangay coverage map

All {{ barangay_count }} barangays drawn from PSA boundary data, shaded by coverage, at-risk share, or child density. Works offline if there is no connection.

{{ icon('package', 20) }}

Vaccine inventory

Batch-level stock with expiry tracking, reorder alerts, and the request workflow between barangay health workers and the RHU.

{{ icon('bar-chart', 20) }}

Coverage analytics

Coverage by barangay, by vaccine, and year on year. Filterable to any month and exportable for LGU reporting.

{{ icon('baby', 20) }}

Child records

Every child's full dose history against the EPI schedule, showing what was given, what is due, and what is overdue.

{{ icon('users', 20) }}

Role-based access

A health worker sees her own barangay, RHU personnel see the municipality, and the administrator sees totals but never a child's record. Every change is logged.

Ready to sign in?

ImmunoVision is for authorised San Jacinto RHU and Barangay Health Worker accounts. If you do not have an account yet, contact your RHU administrator to get access. The public coverage data is available without signing in.
